Why government digital portals feel broken.

Each department built its own portal, data format, and login system. Citizens resubmit the same documents repeatedly, while officials lack a single view.

HOW EKATRA WORKS

An interoperability layer, not another portal.

Six core building blocks let departments exchange verified data safely without touching legacy systems.

01

Standards-Based APIs

A unified OpenAPI data-format layer with plug-and-play connectors for both legacy mainframes and modern cloud services.

02

Master Data Management

A single, validated source of truth for citizen and beneficiary records, synchronized automatically across all departments.

03

Consent-Based Sharing

Data moves only when a citizen grants explicit cryptographic permission — every access event is auditable.

04

Federated Identity / SSO

One unified identity backed by DigiLocker and Aadhaar works across every department without repetitive e-KYC.

05

Event Notifications

Status updates anywhere in the pipeline — approvals, grievances, or fund transfers — push instant SMS/WhatsApp alerts.

06

Workflow Orchestration

Configurable, unified application tracking with role-based access, exception handling, and real-time SLA dashboards.

OUTCOMES

Measurable impact for every stakeholder.

Clear, tangible efficiency gains for the three groups interacting with government systems daily.

Citizens & Businesses

Zero duplicate submissions, one unified dashboard to track all applications, and elimination of manual office queues.

Government Officials

A 360° consolidated view of citizen approvals, 70% faster verification turnaround, and automated cross-department handoffs.

IT & Department Teams

Standardized REST microservices instead of bespoke point-to-point links, guaranteed uptime, and tamper-proof audit trails.
